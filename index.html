Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026. This policy explains what the Trace Chrome extension (“Trace”) does with your information.

The short version

Trace stores what you choose to save on your own computer. It does not send your saved content to us or to anyone else. We do not sell data, show ads or track you.

What Trace stores, and where

  • Content you choose to save: text from AI chats and web pages, page titles and links, copies of the pictures you save, YouTube links and time stamps, and the notes, record names and project names you write.

All of this is kept in Chrome’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. When you save a picture, Trace keeps a copy of it on your computer, because many AI image links are private or stop working after a while. If a picture cannot be downloaded, Trace takes a one-time capture of the visible tab and keeps only that picture. Record summaries are written on your computer, by Chrome’s built-in AI when your Chrome has it.

When Trace reads a web page

Trace only runs on a page when you ask it to: when you click the Trace icon or press its keyboard shortcut and choose an option, choose a Trace item in the right-click menu, click “Open” on a saved item to go back to it, or choose “Send to an AI chat”, which opens a new chat and attaches the record you picked. It then reads the messages, text, image or link you select, or finds the saved messages to highlight them. It does not read pages in the background or record your browsing history.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: to show the selection tools on the page you are on, only after you click Trace.
  • Access to chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, grok.com, x.com/i/grok and chat.deepseek.com: only so that, when you open a saved item, Trace can scroll to the saved messages in that chat and highlight them, and so that it can attach a record to a new chat when you ask it to.
  • Access to googleusercontent.com and oaiusercontent.com: only to keep a copy of a picture you chose to save from Gemini or ChatGPT, so it still shows in your library later. The copy stays on your computer.
  • contextMenus: to add “Save … to Trace” to the right-click menu.
  • storage and unlimitedStorage: to keep your records on your computer.

Payments

Trace is free and does not collect any payment information.

Your choices

You can export a backup, import it on another computer, delete any record, or delete all data from Trace’s Settings page. Uninstalling Trace removes its stored data from Chrome.

Children

Trace is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

Trace’s use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
